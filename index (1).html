About me

Hi, I’m Matej

Fitness has been part of most of my life. Alongside almost 15 years working in law, I have worked as a trainer the whole time. Over the years I have learned about training, nutrition and supplementation, and I have applied and tested that knowledge through my own experience.

I have also competed in the Body Classic category at a bodybuilding competition in Croatia. That experience further shaped my approach to training, discipline, nutrition and understanding the body.

Over time I began to see more and more how much our lifestyle affects our physical condition, energy and health. A fast pace, stress, too little movement, poor eating habits and neglecting our own needs often distance us from our own bodies.

Working with people, I often notice that they put themselves last. Work, family and daily obligations become the priority, while looking after their own health gets postponed.

Through one-to-one work and training, I try to help people change that. Movement, nutrition and daily habits are not separate parts of life; together they shape how we feel and function.

I believe it is important to understand your own body, recognise its needs and learn how to look after it in the long term. That is why my goal is for what we do in training to become part of everyday life, not just an hour spent in the gym.

I am a law graduate by profession and a certified fitness trainer from the Fitness učilište in Zagreb. In my work I combine many years of training experience, continuous learning and personal experience to help people build a sustainable approach to movement and self-care.

Outside work I am a husband and a father of four. That reminds me every day how much energy, health and self-care matter for the quality of everyday life.

Let the way you look be the clearest sign of your healthy habits.

Frequently asked questions

I’ve never trained before. Is this for me?+

Yes. We adapt the programme to your current level and learn the technique from the very first session. That’s what I’m here for: you don’t have to work out how to start or what to do. I guide you step by step.

Where do the sessions take place?+

Sessions take place at THE Fitness Borovje gym, Letovanićka ulica 25, 10000 Zagreb.

What’s the difference between 1:1 and small group training?+

In a 1:1 session the trainer is focused only on you, and the training is adapted to your goals, abilities and fitness.

In a small group you train with one or two other people: a friend, a partner, an acquaintance or someone you’d like to spend more time with. With the trainer’s guidance you get instructions and corrections, and training together brings extra motivation and a lower price per person.
